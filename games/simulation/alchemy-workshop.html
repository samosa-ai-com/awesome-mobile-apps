⚗️ The Three Levers

Heat drives potency but burns purity if too high. Time deepens extraction; too long wastes coin. Apparatus refines purity and stabilizes, but the finest glass costs dearly.

"A good alchemist does not chase perfection — she meets the contract." — Aldric

🎯 Reading Targets

Each commission lists Potency ≥, Purity ≥, and Cost ≤. Meet 1 for ⭐, 2 for ⭐⭐, all 3 for ⭐⭐⭐. Cost is in drams; lower is cheaper.

"The client pays for what they asked, not for your masterpiece."

🧪 Trial vs Assay

Run Trial simulates in your own crucible — no penalty, no stars. Submit Batch sends to the Guild Assayer; it is logged and scored. Use trials to bracket the sweet spot.

🔓 Unlocking

Commissions 1–3 are open. Earn at least ⭐ on a commission to unlock the next. Commission 12, the Panacea, requires 24 total stars.

💾 Save Codes

Your stars and ledger live in this browser. Use Copy Save Code in Ledger to back up or move devices. Paste it back with Import.

Structure & Features

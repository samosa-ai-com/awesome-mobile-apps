🌻 How to Farm

Cozy Farm Grid is a quick, tactile farming loop. Every level has its own crops, field size and coin target.

🌱
Plant: Select a seed in the shop (costs coins), then tap any dashed empty plot.
⏳
Grow: Green bar fills. Each crop has its own grow time (6–16s).
✨
Harvest: When plot says Ready!, tap to collect to your bag. Don't wait too long!
🥀
Wilt: Ready crops wilt if not harvested in time (red bar). Tap wilted plot to clear.
🐦‍⬛
Crows: Randomly land on ready crops. Tap the plot within 3 seconds to shoo (+2🪙) or it wilts!
💰
Sell: Tap Sell All to turn bag items into coins. Reach the level target to level up!

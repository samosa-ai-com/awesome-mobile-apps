How to Play

Goal: Meet the coin quota in each of 8 phases. You have 3 spins per phase.

Spin: Drops 6–8 random tokens from your bag onto the 4×4 workbench. Neighbors (including diagonals) trigger bonuses and chain together.

Draft: Between spins, pick 1 of 3 quirky tokens to permanently add to your bag. Build synergies!

Contractors: Each has a starting kit and a house rule that buffs one token type.

Tips: Clover gives +2 to every neighbor. Piggy loves coins. Chest wants variety. Star loves empty space. Mushroom hates stars.

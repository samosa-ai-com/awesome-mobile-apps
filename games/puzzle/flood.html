How to Play

Start at the ★ top-left. Your region is all connected tiles of the same color.

1. Tap a color — your region recolors to it.
2. Any neighbor tiles that already are that color get swallowed.
3. Repeat until the whole board is one color.
4. You have a fixed move limit — plan ahead!
Lv 1 — 8×8 • 4 colors14 moves
Lv 2 — 10×10 • 4 colors18 moves
Lv 3 — 12×12 • 5 colors22 moves
Lv 4 — 14×14 • 5 colors25 moves
Lv 5+ — 14×14 • 6 colors26 → 24 moves

Tip: Pick colors that touch the most border tiles. Use Undo to experiment!

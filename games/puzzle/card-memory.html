🃏 How to Play

Flip pairs to match

🎯 Goal

Flip two cards at a time. If they match, they stay face-up and you score a pair. Find all pairs to win.

👆 How to Play

  • Tap a card to flip it — large touch targets, no zoom needed
  • Flip a second card to check for a match
  • Matched pairs stay up with a green highlight
  • Mismatched cards flip back — remember their positions!

⭐ Scoring

Fewer moves + faster time = more stars. Best moves and time saved per difficulty. Use Restart to replay the same layout or New Game for a fresh shuffle.

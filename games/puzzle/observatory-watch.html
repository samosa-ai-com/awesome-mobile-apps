Duty rules

Pick a target, then click a time slot to set its start; one exposure fills consecutive slots. Each target must sit fully inside its window with starting moonlight at or under the limit. Switching instruments needs at least one calibration gap between targets. After a zero-risk pass, beating the published compact span with no calibration help earns three stars.

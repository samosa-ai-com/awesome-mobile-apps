How: Drag anywhere on the sea or use the slider to rotate the amber beam. Ships inside the glowing cone are guided toward the harbor (center bottom). Keep the beam on them until they dock. Crash or miss = -1 life. Combo grows with consecutive rescues.

Large drag area • No horizontal scroll • Best score & level saved

Tips

• Beam: 30° cone from lantern (bottom center). Drag left/right to rotate -60° to +60°.
• Guide: Ships in beam slowly steer toward beam center and avoid rocks. Hold until harbor.
• Harbor: Glowing zone at bottom center (80px wide). Only rescues there count.
• Rocks: Dark obstacles — guided ships will try to steer around, but direct hits still crash.
• Combo: Each rescue +1 combo, score = 100 × combo. Miss/crash resets combo.
• Levels: Every 5 rescues = level up, faster ships + more rocks.
• Boost: Tap ⚡ for brief wider beam (5s cooldown).

HOW TO PLAY

  • 1. Place 3 sensors — Tap clear cells. Ridges (≋) block placement. Tap a sensor again to remove it.
  • 2. Read warp — Each sensor shows warped distance = Euclidean + ridgeCount×1.5. RidgeCount = ridges crossed by straight line to beacon.
  • 3. Minimize energy — Energy = sum of 3 readings. Lower = better triangulation. PAR is target for 3★.
  • 4. Tap beacon cell — After 3 sensors, tap any clear cell to guess. Correct + low energy = more stars.
  • Stars: 1★ correct, 2★ if energy ≤ PAR×1.25, 3★ if energy ≤ PAR. Wrong = 0★.
  • Tools: UNDO removes last sensor, HINT reveals quadrant/row, RESET clears. Archive codes save progress via btoa(JSON.stringify(progress)) in localStorage.

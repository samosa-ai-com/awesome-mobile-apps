HOW TO PLAY

• Move ◀ ▶ — slide piece
• Rotate ↻ / ↺ — Up / Z / X
• Soft drop ▼ — faster fall (+1 per cell)
• Hard drop ⤓ — instant lock (+2 per cell)
• Hold ◇ / H — stash one piece
• Clear 1/2/3/4 rows = 100/300/500/800 × level
• Every 10 lines → level up, drop faster
• Game over when new piece can't spawn

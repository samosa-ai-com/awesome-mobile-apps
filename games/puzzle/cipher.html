FIELD GUIDE & STRUCTURE

HOW TO PLAY

  • 1. Select coded: Tap any red letter tile in the message or in the Coded Alphabet grid.
  • 2. Assign plain: Tap a letter in Your Key (black grid) to map it. All instances update instantly.
  • 3. Crack it: When every coded letter is correctly mapped, the message auto-solves.
  • Tap another coded letter to change selection. Tap same plain letter again to clear a mapping.

FEATURES INCLUDED

  • TIMER 3-minute race per message. Bar turns red under 30s. Time bonus on solve.
  • HINT Reveals one random unsolved letter. Costs 20 points & counts in stats.
  • TRACKING Correct / Wrong picks counted on every assignment across all messages.
  • PERSIST Messages decoded, total score, picks & hints saved in localStorage.
  • GRIDS Two 7-column letter grids with large 42px touch targets, no horizontal scroll.
  • PAPER LOOK Detective desk with ruled paper, redacted header, case file styling.
  • CLEAR Wipes current mappings without changing the cipher.
  • NEXT Skips to a new scrambled saying (no penalty).

STRUCTURE

  • Header: Decoded count, total score, countdown timer.
  • Paper: Cipher tiles (code on top, guess below) grouped by word.
  • Coded Alphabet: Shows current guess under each cipher letter; green when solved.
  • Your Key: Plain A-Z; dims letters already used elsewhere.
  • Controls: Hint / Clear / Next + stats row.

TIPS

  • Look for single-letter words → usually A or I.
  • Frequent 3-letter words → THE, AND, YOU.
  • Apostrophes help: DON'T, IT'S.

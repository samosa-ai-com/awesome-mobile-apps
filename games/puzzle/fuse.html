How to Play

• Wires: 3-6 colored wires per level. One is correct.
• Clues: Either name the correct color (“CUT RED”) or exclude others (“ALL EXCEPT BLUE ARE TRAPPED”).
• Tap to cut: Right wire = +100 pts + time bonus, next level. Wrong = -1 heart.
• Timer: Starts at 8.0s, -0.3s per level (min 2.5s). Timeout = -1 heart.
• Hearts: 3 total. Game over at 0.
• Hint: Highlights clue keywords (3s cooldown).

How to Play

1. Aim: Tap ◀ LEFT / RIGHT ▶ or swipe on the lane to set angle (-30° to +30°). Yellow line shows your path.

2. Roll: Tap 🎳 ROLL BALL. Ball launches with physics and knocks pins with chain reactions.

3. Score: Fallen pins = your score (0–10). Strike = all 10 down!

4. Restart: Tap ↻ RESTART for a new game. Best score persists.

Tip: Center aim (0°) is most consistent. Edges risk the gutter!

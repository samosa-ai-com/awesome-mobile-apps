How to play

• Set Aim (-30° to +30°), Power and Curl then tap THROW.
• Curl makes the stone break left/right late in its slide — great for going around guards.
• Hold SWEEP while your stone is moving to make it glide farther and straighter.
• Each end: 8 stones total (4 per team, alternating). Closest to the button scores. First to win most ends wins the match (4 ends).

HOW TO PLAY — COACH'S BOARD

Goal

Each end you have 3 red stones. Slide them from the bottom hack toward the button (top centre). Beat the fixed gold opponent stones. Closest to button scores.

Scoring

  • Only one side scores per end. Count your red stones closer than the opponent's closest gold.
  • 0–3 points per end. Guards can be knocked, bumped or used.
  • Collisions are real — use them to move guards or freeze to them.

Stars & Par

  • ★★★ = max points AND total power ≤ par
  • ★★ = 2+ points within par+20
  • ★ = at least 1 point
  • Lower power = better. Don't blast every throw.

Controls

  • ANGLE −30° to +30° — aim left/right. POWER 12–100 — energy.
  • THROW to slide. UNDO removes last throw. RESET restarts end.
  • Drag on the ice to aim too. Dotted line = preview.
  • COACH DEMO loads suggested angle/power for this throw.

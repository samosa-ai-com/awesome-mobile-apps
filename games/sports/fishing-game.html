How to Play

1. Cast Timing
Tap CAST → meter moves. Tap again in PINK for Perfect cast (trophy fish chance ↑).

2. Read the Bobber
Wait for gentle bobs → small nibbles (ignore!) → BIG DIP + HOOK! That's the bite.

3. Hook-Set
Tap HOOK fast! Perfect <0.3s = +30 pts & bigger fish. Early tap = fish spooked.

4. Scoring
Each fish: base points + size bonus (cm × 0.6) + hook bonus. Fill 60s with as many as you can!

How to Play

• You see one big card. Guess if the next card is higher or lower.
• Correct: Streak +1, multiplier +0.2 (x1.0 → x6.0 max), score +10×multiplier. New card becomes current.
• Equal: Push — no score, streak stays, next card revealed.
• Wrong: Run ends. Best score saved.
• Empty deck: 52 cards total — if you clear the deck, run ends with bonus!
• Tip: 7 is the middle — higher/lower is 50/50 there.

Grid-Digging Crawler

Tap adjacent cells (up/down/left/right) to crack 🪨 then mine 💎. Fill the quota before bats get you. 🏮 Lanterns are safe zones — bats can't hurt you there.

How to play: Start on a lantern. Tap a neighboring rock to crack it, tap again to mine and move in. Collect minerals to fill quota. Bats patrol every ~0.8s — stay on lanterns to dodge. You have 3 hearts.

Large tap targets • No horizontal scroll • Best score & level saved

Guide & Tips

• Adjacent only: You can only crack/mine the 4 cells next to you.
• Crack → Mine: First tap cracks 🪨, second mines and moves you in. Minerals give 10-100 pts.
• Bats 🦇: Patrol back-and-forth. If a bat moves onto you (or you onto it) you lose 1 heart — unless you're on 🏮.
• Lanterns 🏮: Glowing safe zones. Plan hops between them.
• Quota: Level 1 needs 8 minerals, +4 each level. Fill it to level up.
• Hearts: 3 total. 1.2s invulnerability after hit.
• Hint: Highlights a nearby mineral.

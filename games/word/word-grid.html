CODE 5SIX TRIES • FIVE LETTERS

How to play

Guess the secret 5-letter word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a valid word.

After each guess, tiles color to show how close you were:

W Green — right letter, right spot
I Gold — right letter, wrong spot
X Gray — letter not in word

Duplicate rule: If the answer has one L and you guess two Ls, only one can be green/gold — the extra is gray. Colors are assigned green first, then gold from remaining letters.

Use the on-screen keyboard or your physical keyboard. Press Enter to submit, ⌫ to delete.

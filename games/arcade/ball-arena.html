How to play

• Move with joystick or WASD. Bigger = slower but stronger.
• Eat pellets (small dots) and any cell 12% smaller than you.
• Split (blue) divides cells to chase — needs 36 mass, 8 max cells, merges after 6s.
• Eject (yellow) shoots mass forward, useful to feed or escape.
• Viruses (spiky green) split you if you're big — avoid or shoot them.
• Modes: Team = 3 colors, Survival = 3 lives, Zone = shrinking circle, Storm = pellet showers, Outpost = capture points, Boss = giant siege.

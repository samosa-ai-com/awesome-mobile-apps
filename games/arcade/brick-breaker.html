How to Play

• Drag near the bottom launcher to set angle.
• Release to launch balls one by one.
• Each hit = -1 on block number.
• Green +1 pickups add a ball next round.
• Every 3 levels you get +2 bonus balls.
• Blocks descend 1 row per round.
• Don't let them touch the red danger line.

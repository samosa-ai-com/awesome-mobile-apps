🎉 Tinik Hop Fiesta

Tap to leap as bamboo poles snap open & shut to the rhythm. Don’t get caught!

  • 🥢
    Poles snap on the beatTwo bamboo poles lie across the street. They open (gap wide) then snap shut (touching) every beat. Watch the beat bar at the top.
  • 🦶
    Tap to jump at the right momentWhen poles are open you can stand between them. When they close you must be in the air — tap JUMP just before they snap shut.
  • ⭐
    Score for clean clearancesEach successful snap = +1. Perfect timing (jump on the beat) = +2 and combo. Miss = lose a heart. 3 hearts total.
  • ⚡
    Levels quickenEvery 8 points BPM rises (+8). Poles snap faster, windows get tighter. Survive as long as you can!
💡 Rhythm tip

Listen to the hop “pop” and watch the white dot. Jump when the dot hits the right edge — that’s the snap moment. Early is safer than late.

Implementation note

Collision is checked only at the “snap” instant: if gap < 28px and player is on ground → hit. Otherwise clearance. This keeps rhythm fair and predictable.

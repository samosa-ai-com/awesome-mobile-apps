Goal

Climb 12 peaks from Gale Hollow to Storm Crown. Land on platforms, collect ◆ shards, reach the green SUMMIT. Each peak has 18 platforms + summit.

Platforms

Solid — stable. Springy (blue) — super bounce. Moving (amber) — carries you sideways. Crumbling (red) — collapses 0.4s after landing!

Wind

Gusts push you left/right. Watch the WIND meter. Gusts come every 3–5s. Higher peaks = stronger wind.

Leapers

Scout — best steering (tight air control). Gale — powerful dash, short cooldown. Feather — long glide (hold JUMP in air to float).

Controls

◀ ▶ steer • ⤒ JUMP tap to jump, hold in air to glide • ⚡ DASH burst (cooldown) • Keyboard: Arrow keys / A-D, Space/W, Shift

Stars & Persistence

Each peak awards up to 3★: 1 for summit, 1 for ≥50% shards, 1 for beating par time. Best times and total shards save automatically on this phone.

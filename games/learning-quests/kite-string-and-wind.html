📖 Flight Manual

Wind is messy. Your kite feels every gust — unless you filter it. Pick the right handling like a real flight computer.

🎮 How to fly

  • Touch-drag anywhere on the sky to steer. Kite follows your finger with soft inertia.
  • Fly through paper rings drifting down. Center = perfect, edge = ok, miss = break streak.
  • 30s timed wind. Build streaks for bonus points.

🎛️ Handling modes

  • ANSWER-NOW No filter. Every gust shoves you instantly. Best for steady, spaced gusts (L1).
  • DEBOUNCED 250ms Waits until gusts stop, then takes the last one. Collapses bursts into one move. Best for burst storms (L2, L5).
  • THROTTLED 300ms Allows max one gust per 300ms, drops the rest. Smooths rapid flutter (L3, L4).

🌬️ Lessons

  • L1 Steady Breeze — single gust every 900ms → immediate
  • L2 Burst Storm — 4 gusts in 70ms bursts → debounce
  • L3 Rapid Flutter — gust every 110ms → throttle
  • L4 Mixed Front — bursts + flutter → throttle
  • L5 Boss Gale — chaotic everything → debounce

⭐ Scoring

Ring = 100pts + streak bonus (streak×20). 3+ streak = clean line. Miss breaks streak. Earn stars: 500/900/1300 pts. Stars unlock next lesson. Best scores saved on this phone.

Tip: Watch the wind bar at bottom — it shows filtered wind force. If kite jitters, you picked wrong mode!

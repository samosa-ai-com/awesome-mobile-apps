How to Play

Goal: Survive the dungeon by judging live JS expressions.

1. A monster appears with an expression like [] == ![] — this is real JavaScript.

2. Tap TRUE if it evaluates to true, FALSE otherwise. You have 7s (10s for bosses).

3. Correct: You damage the monster. Mobs die in 1 hit, bosses need 3. You gain score (100 base + streak bonus).

4. Combo: Every 3 correct in a row arms a CRITICAL — next hit deals double damage and score x1.5 (stacks to x3 at 9 streak).

5. Wrong / timeout: You lose 18 HP (22 vs boss) and your streak resets.

6. Bosses gate progress. Defeat the boss to unlock the next dungeon. Your best score, kills and unlocks persist.

Dungeons:
1. Novice Crypt — basics (== vs ===)
2. Coercion Caverns — type coercion
3. Abyss of NaN — NaN, null, undefined
4. Citadel of Truth — Object.is & edge cases

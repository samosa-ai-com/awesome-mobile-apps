How the mansion remembers

🕯️ The Rule

When you ask for obj.prop, the mansion searches your floor first. If not found, it climbs the stairs to the prototype, then its prototype, until null.

🪑 Furniture = Properties

Each piece of furniture is a property. Tap it to claim "this floor owns it". If the property isn't on your floor, climb to the ancestor floor that owns it.

⦻ Absence

If no floor owns the property, declare Absence. The chain ends at null — the void beyond the attic.

👻 Shadowing

A lower floor can have the same key as an ancestor — it shadows the one above. The lower one wins.

🔍 hasOwnProperty vs in

'x' in obj checks the whole chain. obj.hasOwnProperty('x') checks only your floor.

💡 Hints

You have 3 hints per chapter. Use sparingly — they pulse the correct floor.

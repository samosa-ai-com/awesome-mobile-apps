HOW TO PLAY

Route 4 train types to their color lines: ● Passenger → BLUE • ● Freight → AMBER • ● Mail → GREEN • ● Medical → RED (priority +150 pts).
Tap an incoming train to select it, then tap its color track or color button to ROUTE. Use Signals to open/close lines (red blocks routing). HOLD freezes one train 5s. E-STOP freezes all 3s (−50 pts, once per shift). Beat the shift clock for stars!

    How to Dispatch

    1. Trains enter on the INBOUND track and move toward the junction.
    2. Tap a train to select it (gold outline).
    3. Check its color stripe — that is its home line.
    4. Make sure that line's signal is GREEN, then tap the color track or ROUTE PAD to dispatch.
    5. Correct = +points, Wrong = −50, Missed = −75.
    6. HOLD pauses one train 5s (8s cooldown). E-STOP pauses all 3s (−50, once per shift).
    7. Beat the clock to earn stars and unlock the next timetable.

How to Duel

Outwit two rivals with a fixed purse. Every antique hides its true value.

  • 1. Read the estimate — True value is hidden until the hammer falls. Some lots are traps, some are bargains.
  • 2. Raise or Pass — Tap +$50 / $100 / $250 / $500 to raise. Pass folds you out of this lot.
  • 3. Rivals react — Victor bluffs often, Silas only bids when he loves it. Watch their status.
  • 4. Win the lot — When both rivals fold, you pay the hammer price. Profit = True Value − Paid.
  • 5. Don't overpay — Overpaying cuts profit and drains your $5,000 purse. You can't bid what you can't afford.
  • 6. Hammer drama — If no one raises, the hammer creeps: Going once → Going twice → Sold! with sound & haptics.

Structure & Persistence

Signal Voyage is 12 nights aboard the deep-space relay Errant. Each night you read a scene, allocate limited bandwidth, pick an operator, and choose an action. Hidden stats shift silently and thread health decides your ending.

  • Nights: 12 sequential scenes. You cannot replay a night without starting a new voyage. Progress auto-saves.
  • Hidden stats: Integrity, Trust, Insight (0–100). Choices nudge them; you see hints, not numbers, until the finale.
  • Bandwidth threads: 3 points per night to split among Helios Relay / Ghost Signal / Crew Heart. Unfunded threads decay -4 each night.
  • Operator picks: Vela (Comms +Relay), Kai (Engineer +Ghost), Reyes (Medic +Crew). Operator adds +6 to their thread that night.
  • Endings (5): Homeward, Contact, Heartbound, Lost Signal, Synthesis — unlocked by thread health thresholds at Night 12.
  • Persistence: Saved in localStorage: signalVoyage_v2 (current voyage), signalVoyage_archive (voyage history), signalVoyage_endings (endings unlocked). Nights cleared and endings persist across app restarts. Use Reset to wipe current voyage only; archive stays unless you clear it.

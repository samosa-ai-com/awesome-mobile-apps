Structure & Key Features

Mobile-first pocket builder for 360–428px portrait. No horizontal scroll, 48px+ touch targets, 16px readable text.

  • 12 Scenarios: Each city has unique size (6×6 to 8×8), starting reserves, 8-year limit, and goals for Happiness / Reserves / Pollution.
  • Zoning: Homes (⌂), Shops ($), Works (⚙), Park (♣). Costs deducted instantly. Adjacency matters: Parks boost nearby Homes, Works hurt Homes, Shops earn more near Homes.
  • Yearly Budget: Income from zones minus upkeep + policy costs = net. Reserves carry over. End Year to advance.
  • Policies: Tax Relief, Green Deal, Metro Lines, Forge Subsidy. Toggle any combo; effects stack and show live in meters.
  • Bulldoze: Clear tool costs $100, removes block for replanning.
  • Goals: Must meet all three at year 8. Stars: 1 for win, +1 for high happiness, +1 for low pollution.
  • Replay vs Reference: Ghost overlays reference layout; Replay modal compares stats and layout diff.
  • Persist & Export: Auto-saves to localStorage. Archive view exports full JSON (progress + city histories) for backup.

Tip: Early cities teach parks. Later cities need tight budgeting — don’t over-zone Works before you can afford Green Deal.

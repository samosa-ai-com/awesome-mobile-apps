    HOW TO PLAY

  • Timer: 3.2s at start → 1.4s at max. Bar color warns you (teal → yellow → red blink).
  • Scoring: 100 base + time left (0-100) + streak×25. Example: 2.8s left on 5 streak = 100+87+125=312
  • Difficulty: Every 5 correct → harder words (Easy → Medium → Hard → Expert). Timer shortens 150ms per level.
  • Storage: Best score, streak & level saved locally (localStorage). No login.
  • Particles: Canvas confetti on correct, screen shake on wrong.
  • Targets: All buttons ≥56px, 16px+ text, no zoom needed.

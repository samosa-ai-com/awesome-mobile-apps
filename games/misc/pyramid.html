Build vs Topple

Left half / BUILD stacks your stepped pyramid (4-3-2-1) to 10 stones.
Right half / HURL hurls stones to drain rival stability.
Win a round by completing first or toppling the rival. First to 2 rounds wins the duel.

Large tap targets • No horizontal scroll • Best results saved on this device

How to Play

• BUILD (left): Each tap adds 1 stone to your pyramid. Fill all 10 to win the round.
• HURL (right): Each tap throws a stone at the rival. Each hit -1 stability (10→0). At 0 they topple and you win the round.
• Rival does the same to you — watch your stability!
• First to 2 rounds wins the match. Fastest wins are saved.
• Tip: If rival is close to 10, hurl to stop them. If you're close, keep building.
